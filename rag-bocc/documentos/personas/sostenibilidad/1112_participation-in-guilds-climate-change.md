Lobbying and trade associations – Climate alignment

Banco de Occidente recognizes the importance of the financial sector in the transition
toward a low-carbon economy and the relevance of promoting spaces for dialogue and
coordination with public and private actors to contribute to sustainable development,
climate change management, and the strengthening of sustainable finance.

In line with its sustainability strategy and climate commitments, the Bank participates in
trade associations, sector initiatives, and institutional forums that promote practices
aligned with the Paris Agreement and the principles of corporate sustainability.

The Bank participates in technical and sectoral forums related to sustainable finance,
climate regulation, and ESG risk management, contributing to public–private dialogue and
the development of initiatives aligned with the climate transition and the strengthening of
the financial sector.

Governance framework for regulatory affairs and industry relations.

Banco de Occidente has guidelines for participation in industry associations and the
monitoring of regulatory affairs and public policy related to sustainability and climate
change.

The Sustainability Management leads the technical evaluation of the associations,
initiatives, and participation spaces in which the Bank is involved, in coordination with the
Institutional Relations, Legal, Risk areas, and the involved vice presidencies, according to
the nature of each initiative.

As part of this process, the following is evaluated:

   •   The alignment of the public positions of the associations with the Bank’s climate
       and sustainability strategy.
   •   The contribution of such associations to topics related to climate change, energy
       transition, and sustainable finance.
   •   The regulatory, reputational, and ESG risks associated with industry participation.
   •   The consistency of the initiatives and activities carried out with corporate
       commitments and the principles of the Paris Agreement.

The results of this analysis are presented to the Sustainability Committee, the body
responsible for validating recommendations related to the continuity, adjustment,
conditioning, or reevaluation of the Bank’s participation in such associations, strengthening
executive-level oversight on public policy and sustainability matters.

Management of misalignments against climatic positions


<!-- página 3 -->

   Banco de Occidente periodically monitors the public positions, regulatory agendas,
   and activities carried out by the industry associations in which it participates, with the
   objective of verifying their consistency with the Bank’s climate commitments and the
   principles of the Paris Agreement.

   The Bank conducts an annual review of the climate alignment of relevant industry
   associations and sector initiatives, considering their public positions, activities,
   regulatory contributions, and participation in topics related to climate change and
   sustainability.

   When potential misalignments with the institutional climate strategy are identified,
   the Sustainability Management develops a technical analysis that may include:

       •   Dialogue and feedback processes with the corresponding association.
       •   Requests for review or clarification regarding public positions.
       •   Escalation of the analysis to the Sustainability Committee.
       •   Reevaluation of the Bank’s continued participation in the association,
           considering reputational, regulatory, and strategic impacts.

The Bank may reconsider, condition, or terminate its participation in associations whose
positions are persistently inconsistent with its climate strategy and the principles of the
Paris Agreement.

Monitoring, evaluation, and reporting

The Bank conducts periodic reviews of its participation in industry associations and
institutional spaces related to sustainability and climate change. The monitoring includes
the review of objectives, activities, public positions, and sector contributions of each
association.

Information related to associations, public positions, identified risks, and participation
outcomes is consolidated and documented periodically for the purposes of monitoring,
traceability, and internal decision-making.

The findings and conclusions derived from this analysis are integrated into internal
sustainability and corporate governance processes, strengthening decision-making and
alignment with international ESG standards and best practices.

Political neutrality

Banco de Occidente maintains a neutral position regarding political matters and does not
make contributions to electoral campaigns or political lobbying organizations in the
jurisdictions where it operates.
