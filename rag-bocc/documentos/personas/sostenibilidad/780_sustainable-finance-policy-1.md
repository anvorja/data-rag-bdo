                          1. Sustainable Finance Policy

1.1 Overview:


At Banco de Occidente, we adopt and promote environmental, social, and governance
(ESG) principles across our activities, as well as in the financing of sustainable operations
that contribute to a responsible environmental transition, strengthening our clients’
competitiveness while improving environmental outcomes and societal well-being.

From this perspective, we seek to provide financing solutions that enable our clients to make
decisions incorporating ESG criteria into their economic activities and projects. The positive
impact that can be achieved through this type of financing is significant. We are not only
contributing to Colombia’s economic growth and stability but also fostering the adoption of
more responsible and environmentally sustainable business practices.

In an economy transitioning toward a low-carbon model, the role of the financial sector is
fundamental. We have both the capacity and the responsibility to channel resources toward
projects and companies that promote sustainability, climate change mitigation, and
adaptation. By offering sustainable financing solutions, we support innovation and the
uptake of clean technologies, contributing to a more resilient and sustainable economy over
the long term.

As a financial institution, we are committed to investing in the development of communities
and municipalities, as this not only contributes to the economic growth of the country, but
also improves the quality of life of its inhabitants. This financial support aims to promote
social projects that address the needs of education, health, access to essential services,
housing, etc., for various interest groups such as women, children, the elderly, minority
groups, among others.

Our commitment to sustainability is not only reflected in our internal policies and practices,
but also in the way we engage with our customers and the community at large. We are
working closely with companies of all sizes and individual customers to help them achieve
their business goals sustainably and responsibly. Offering sustainable financing lines is
more than a business strategy, it is a commitment to the future of Colombia and the planet.


1.2 Objective

To establish the general guidelines that enable us to play an active role in the development
of a more equitable, inclusive, and environmentally and socially responsible economy
through the incorporation of ESG criteria into the financing of projects, assets, and working
capital for clients within the Corporate Banking, Business Banking, SME Banking,
Government, Institutional Banking, Structured Finance Unit, and Retail Banking segments.

1.3 Scope of application

These guidelines apply to all individual and corporate clients belonging to the business
segments1 and cover all operations in which we have a presence, identifying clients from
the various economic sectors vulnerable to climate change, those that generate greater
environmental and social impacts due to the development of their activities and those that
may represent greater opportunities through the implementation of mitigation and adaptation
measures.


1.4 Environmental and Social Standards, Frameworks and Initiatives Adopted

Banco de Occidente adheres to the following environmental and social standards,
frameworks, and initiatives:

    •    Asobancaria Green Protocol.

    •    Asobancaria Social Protocol.

    •    United Nations Global Compact.

    •    United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

    •    United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative (UNEP FI) – Principles
         for Responsible Banking.

    •    Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD).

    •    Dow Jones Sustainability Index.

    •    Finance for Biodiversity Foundation.


1.5 Our Sustainability Strategy


Our sustainability strategy is designed to effectively integrate sustainability principles across
all areas of our operations, aligning our practices with environmental, social, and
governance (ESG) principles.

This strategy seeks not only to comply with current regulations and stakeholder
expectations, but also to lead by example within the financial sector by generating a positive
impact on society and the environment.

The strategic pillars through which we drive our ESG initiatives, under our purpose of
“Making people’s development, business growth, and the country’s transformation a reality,”
are:

Governance/Economic Aspect

    •    Transparency: We strengthen corporate governance with ethical and responsible
         practices.

    •    Profitable growth: We promote sustainable businesses that contribute to the
         economic, social and environmental development of the country.

Social Aspect

    •    Our people: We foster a work environment where we promote the comprehensive
         development of talent.

    •    Generation of social value: We create meaningful experiences for our customers
         and generate alliances for the development of the regions.


Environmental Aspect

    •    Our Blue Planet: We contribute to the mitigation and adaptation of climate change.

1.6 Sustainable business financing

At Banco de Occidente, we integrate environmental, social and governance (ESG)
considerations at the core of our financial decision-making. Through our financing strategies,
we support the transition to a low-carbon economy, strengthening our clients’
competitiveness while generating a positive impact for our stakeholders. With this vision, we
offer financing solutions that enable our clients to integrate ESG criteria into their business
activities and projects. Beyond dedicated sustainable finance products, virtually any lending
product within our portfolio may qualify as a sustainable financing solution when aligned with
the criteria established by the Bank (see Annex 1 – Sustainable Portfolio).

With the aim of mobilizing resources in the different segments of the business2 for the
financing of economic activities, projects and assets that contribute significantly to the
environment and society, we have developed our taxonomy based on the Green Taxonomy
of Colombia, the taxonomy provided by IDB Invest and the Social Protocol of Asobancaria.

1.7 Commitment to our customers

To build strong and trusted relationships and promote a balanced approach to economic
growth, environmental stewardship, and social well-being, Banco de Occidente adopts an
integrated approach that enables us to mitigate sustainability-related risks and capitalize on
sustainability-related opportunities, ensuring responsible and resilient operations in the face
of global challenges. Accordingly, we seek to support clients through our Corporate Banking,
Business Banking, SME Banking, Government, Institutional Banking, Structured Finance,
and Retail Banking segments in identifying and understanding ESG-related risks and
opportunities through the following actions:

   •   Providing information through our website regarding sustainable products and
       services, including eligible financing categories, our current sustainable finance
       portfolio, and the characteristics of the products offered.

   •   Maintaining a commitment to financial education, regulatory updates, and the
       implementation of technologies that facilitate our clients' adaptation to evolving
       market, regulatory, and sustainability-related requirements.

   •   Developing training and capacity-building initiatives aimed at Corporate, Business,
       and SME Banking clients, as well as strategic clients, with the objective of
       strengthening decision-making capabilities, enhancing risk management practices,
       fostering sustainable growth, and providing key tools to improve business
       competitiveness through updates on strategic and operational topics.


1.8 Integration of environmental, social and governance (ESG) criteria


Globally, there is a growing recognition of the need to incorporate environmental, social and
governance (ESG) factors into financial decision-making processes. At Banco de Occidente,
environmental considerations include risks related to climate change, sustainable
management of natural resources and the environmental footprint. Social factors
encompass issues such as human rights, working conditions and social equity. Accordingly,
we have established guidelines and implemented practices aimed at ensuring that our direct
lending activities generate a positive impact on both society and the environment.

In addition, to comply with legal obligations and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Know
Your Customer (KYC) requirements, all Retail Banking and Corporate/Business Banking
transactions are screened against restricted lists related to money laundering, drug
trafficking, terrorism, and other illegal activities. Furthermore, detailed information is
collected regarding customer identity, source of funds, economic activity, and other relevant
aspects, recognizing transparency and ethical conduct as key governance considerations.

Within our Retail Banking segment, the Bank integrates ESG criteria into its financing
schemes through the offering of products designed to encourage sustainable consumption
decisions among individual customers. This integration is primarily achieved through
financing products dedicated to sustainable activities that meet the criteria established under
the Bank’s Sustainable and Transition Taxonomy. In this way, ESG factors are explicitly
incorporated into the credit origination process, promoting the allocation of capital toward
activities aligned with environmental transition objectives and sustainable development, in
accordance with the principles established in this Policy.

We are committed to managing the environmental, social, economic and governance risks
related to our lending activity. This approach enables us not only to mitigate potential
adverse impacts but also to direct financial flows toward economic activities that
appropriately manage their impacts on society and the environment.
.
To support this commitment, the Bank has implemented an Environmental and Social Risk
Management System (ESRMS), aligned with our credit approval process, to identify, assess,
and manage environmental and social risks. The EMRS applies to all our commercial
lending operations, including projects evaluated in our Project Finance Unit.

Finally, in line with our commitment, it has been defined not to finance activities with a high
environmental and social impact contained in the Prohibited Activities section of our
Exclusion List. This list includes activities prohibited under applicable national laws and
regulations, as well as those restricted under international conventions and agreements
ratified by the host country, or activities subject to international phase-out commitments or
global prohibitions. Other activities with a high environmental and social impact not
prohibited by Colombian legislation could be financed only if the sum of their balance does
not represent more than 5% of our total portfolio.
