                         DECALOGUE OF SAFETY AND HEALTH AT WORK

       In the Occupational Health and Safety department, we manage the health care of employees
       and their productive capacity, reducing occupational risks through self-care and safe
       practices at work.

                                     OH&S Policy and Regulations

       We are committed to the safety and well-being of our employees, contractors, and anyone
       under our supervision. Our Occupational Health and Safety policy is applicable to all our
       operations and complies with relevant national and international regulations, as well as
       applicable voluntary standards and collective agreements.

       We encourage the consultation and participation of our employees and their representatives
       in the identification of risks, the implementation of preventive measures and the continuous
       improvement of our OSH management system. To this end, we set quantifiable objectives,
       prioritize action plans and promote a culture of prevention in all our areas of work.

       As part of our commitment to sustainability and improving organizational performance, we
       report our OSH progress within the framework of key indicators used in assessments such
       as the Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI). This allows us to continuously evaluate and
       optimize our performance metrics, ensuring responsible management aligned with industry
       best practices.

       Our Executive Management endorses and promotes the implementation of this policy,
       ensuring its integration into our corporate strategy and its alignment with our values of well-
       being and sustainability.

Responsibilities in relation to the Occupational Health and Safety Management System

 In accordance with the commitment of all levels of the organization to Occupational Health
 and Safety, the following responsibilities are defined for the fulfillment of all.

    •   Learn about the policies regarding the OHS SG.
    •   Take care of your overall health and well-being.
    •   Provide clear, truthful and complete information about your state of health.
    •   Immediately report any accidents or incidents at work to your leader.
    •   Participate in the prevention of occupational risks through the COPASST
        committees.
    •   Report the risk conditions detected to your leader.
    •   It complies with the rules, regulations and instructions of the Occupational Health
        and Safety Management System (OHS-MS).
    •   Participate in evacuation drills.

                           Hazards and Risks in the Workplace

 At our bank, we manage occupational health and safety through a structured approach
 based on hazard identification, risk assessment and incident investigation.

 We implement an Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OH&S-MS) based
 on Colombian regulations, guaranteeing a safe work environment for our employees.

 To identify hazards, we carry out periodic inspections at the facilities, surveys of employees
 and analysis of working conditions, in order to detect potential risks.

 We use qualitative and quantitative risk analysis methodologies to prioritize and mitigate
 threats.

 In terms of risk assessment, we apply the risk matrix to classify and manage hazards related
 to ergonomic, psychosocial, biological and physical factors. In addition, we promote
 operational controls and specific action plans to minimize exposure to occupational risks.

 Potential occupational safety and health hazards have been identified:

 Biomechanics Hazards:


    •   Inadequate design of the workstation.
    •   Repetitive movements (fingering).
    •   Prolonged and inappropriate postures.

Psychosocial:

   •   Characteristics of the task and the role.
   •   Labor Demands.
   •   Social relations and leadership.

Physical:

   •   Poor or excessive lighting.
   •   Thermal discomfort (cold or heat).
   •   Noise (perception).

Electrical:

   •   Exposed wiring.
   •   Temporary electrical installations in poor condition.

Mechanical:

   •   Elements or parts of work tools that can generate blows.
   •   Tools in poor condition such as drawers, chairs, among others.

Locative:

   •   Uneven work surfaces.
   •   Unfavorable conditions of order and cleanliness.
   •   Storage systems and insufficient space.

                                  Prevention Measures

To strengthen our Occupational Health and Safety Management System, we have
developed a comprehensive plan that includes the planning and definition of OH&S policies,
indicators and objectives, as well as the development of work plans for the different
surveillance and prevention programs.

These include psychosocial surveillance systems and prevention of musculoskeletal
disorders, industrial safety and hygiene, occupational medicine and reintegration processes,
emergency preparedness and response, strategic road safety plan and public risk
management. In addition, we worked on the Pregnancy and Breastfeeding Support
Program, the communication and training plan in OSH, the strengthening of employee
participation committees such as COPASST, the Coexistence Committee and the Road
Safety Committee, and the program for suppliers and contractors.

According to what has been identified, here are some of the strategies implemented to
reduce the occurrence of occupational accidents and occupational diseases in the Bank:

Biomechanics:

Belonging to the Epidemiological Surveillance System for the Prevention of Musculoskeletal
Disorders, such as:

   •    Design of workstations.
   •    Active and cognitive breaks.
   •    Ergonomic inspections.
   •    Schools on musculoskeletal health.
   •    Training in postural hygiene.
   •    Recommendations on the use of workstations (Desk, computer, keyboard, mouse,

   •    Job analysis.

Belonging to the Psychosocial Epidemiological Surveillance System, such as:

    •   Application of psychosocial risk battery.
    •   Psychosocial inspections.

        promotion and prevention activities).
    •   Secondary prevention: Follow-up of cases, orientation to leaders, monitoring and

Mechanical, Electrical, Physical and Locative:

Belonging to the Industrial Safety program, such as:

    •   Activities for the prevention of incidents and accidents at work.
    •   Safety inspections and training.
    •   Socialization of Fall Standards.
    •   Days of Order and Cleanliness (cleaning and hygiene of the workspace).

                            Design of intervention programs

Currently, the intervention activities associated with the related preventive measures are
established through the hierarchy of risk (source, medium and person), taking into account
the processes, the tasks of the positions and the duration of the working day.

The working schedule is in accordance with the provisions established by Colombian
regulations to date (44 hours per week). Currently, part-time work arrangements are not part
of the employment model due to operational needs.


                    Pregnancy and Breastfeeding Support Program

Our program provides the necessary support during the processes of pregnancy,
breastfeeding and return to work.

The program encourages and facilitates breastfeeding for the Bank's employees, safely and
in a healthy and welcoming work environment.

It applies to pregnant women up to 24 months after the birth of the baby, who are linked to
the bank. Training and talks associated with childcare, infant and maternal care are included.

                  Occupational Health and Safety Disability Reporting


Disability Leave Reports:
From Occupational Safety and Health, the filing is made with the Health Promoter Entity -
EPS to later carry out the capture in the established system.

Alleged Accident or Work Incident in:

According to the OSH Responsibilities, if it is considered that the event may be a work
accident, it must be reported immediately, informing the immediate leader and the
Directorate of Safety and Health at Work -OSH of your area, through the established and
socialized channels.

Health Conditions: Medical Recommendations or Restrictions

The employee must report the recommendation or medical condition (medical support,
among others) to the Bank's occupational health and safety directorate (OHS) to the Bank's
occupational health and safety department.

If the employee wishes to inform or provide management support to Human Talent and/or
the Occupational Health and Safety Directorate (OHS) of the area, he/she must send a
Medical History and attach authorization or informed consent for its review.

              Employee Engagement, Consultation and Communication

We guarantee the participation of our employees in the Occupational Health and Safety
Management System (OH&S-MS) through different consultation, communication and
decision-making mechanisms:

Joint Occupational Health and Safety Committee (COPASST):

   •   It supervises the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OH&S-MS),
       proposes improvements and participates in investigations of occupational accidents.
   •   Representatives on behalf of the collaborators (4) and on the part of the employer
       (4) with their respective alternates, there is a committee per zone, monthly meetings
       are held.

Emergency Operating Committee – COE:

   •   Coordinates prevention and response actions in emergencies.
   •   Made up of leaders of Administrative Resources, Human Talent, Banking Security,
       OSH, among others.

Labor Coexistence Committee:

   •   It prevents workplace harassment, manages complaints and promotes coexistence.
   •   Representatives on behalf of the collaborators (2) and on the part of the employer
       (2) with their respective alternates, there is a committee per zone, quarterly meetings
       are held.

Road Safety Committee:

   •   Generate awareness in the prevention of Road Safety in employees to achieve the
       objectives established in the Strategic Road Safety Plan - PESV.
   •   Raise, design, implement, and measure the actions and/or activities of the work
       plan of the Strategic Road Safety Plan - PESV.
   •   Surveillance and monitoring of compliance with the policy and the Strategic Plan for
       Road Safety.

                Promotion of the Health and Well-being of Employees

We guarantee that our employees have the necessary time to attend medical appointments
and procedures, regardless of whether they are scheduled by their EPS, policy or prepaid
medicine service. Each leader manages these permissions and, when necessary, Human
Talent and OSH provide support to ensure compliance. We offer health promotion programs:


<!-- página 9 -->

   •   Health Week: Annual event with talks and activities on wellness, disease
       prevention and healthy eating.
   •   Blood Donation Day: Spaces in our facilities where health entities facilitate the
       collection of donations.
   •   Executive Check-Up Program: Specialized medical exams for senior management,
       middle management, and management.
   •   Nutritional Challenge: Accompaniment in healthy habits for overweight or obese
       employees.

We offer recreational sports activities:

   •   Bowling Tournament: through calls launched in the cities of Cali, Barranquilla,
       Bogotá, Pereira and Medellín, bowling tournaments were held for registered
       collaborators with the aim of promoting camaraderie and teamwork among
       collaborators.
   •   Family Cinema Days: through calls launched in the cities of Cali, Barranquilla,
       Bogotá and Medellín, film days were held in which each registered collaborator was
       allowed to bring a guest, in this way, we provided a space for collaborators to enjoy
       quality time with their families.
   •   Beach Volleyball Tournament: Through calls launched in the city of Cali, beach
       volleyball championship days were held so that employees could form teams and
       participate; promoting physical activity and sportsmanship.
   •   Volunteering and ecological walks: ecological volunteering activities were carried
       out with WWF to generate an environmental impact by planting trees. These
       activities were carried out through calls for collaborators. These activities reinforce
       commitment to the community and foster a sense of social responsibility among
       employees.
