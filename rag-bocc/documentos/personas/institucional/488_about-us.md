Banco de Occidente

ABOUT US

We are part of Grupo Aval Acciones y Valores. The company was founded in Cali in 1965,
since then we have been fulfilled a development commitment in the country, responding to
the trust of the community, its clients and its shareholders.

We have 53 years of operation and 217 offices in Colombia, as well as subsidiaries in
Panama and Barbados.
